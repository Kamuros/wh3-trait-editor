# Third-party dependencies

`js/vendor/fzstd.js` is the UMD distribution of **fzstd 0.1.1** by Arjun Barrett, distributed under the MIT license. Its full copyright and license notice is in `js/vendor/fzstd-LICENSE.txt`.

- Source: https://github.com/101arrowz/fzstd
- Published package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/fzstd/v/0.1.1
- Purpose: decode Zstandard-compressed language entries from user-selected WH3 packs.

The decoder is bundled locally; the editor does not fetch code from a CDN.
